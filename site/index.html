Kitchen and garden planner

Nothing rots.
Nothing is wasted.

Fallow tracks what's in your pantry, tells you what to cook before it spoils, and plans what to plant to fill the gaps, from dirt to plate and back.

Watch the presentation Try the live demo

The live demo opens instantly on any phone. Want everything? Open the full app (the first load takes about 30 seconds).

Cook what spoils first

Every item gets a use-by estimate, and the meal plan is built from whatever is closest to going off.

Keep the body stocked

Fresh food, calorie staples and protein are tracked as reserves, and readings from the watch shape what to eat.

Plant against the gap

Soil and weather readings decide what can go in the ground now, and scraps go back to the soil as compost.
